# Glossary

Exam Prep Lab is a study site for certification exams. These are the words for what Learners study and what the owner sees about their use of it.

## Studying

**Exam**: A certification test a Learner is preparing for, such as a cloud or Scrum certification. Each Exam has exactly one Bank.
_Avoid_: Test, course, certification (when meaning the study material).

**Bank**: The full set of Questions for one Exam.
_Avoid_: Question set, question pool, dataset.

**Question**: One multiple-choice item in a Bank: a prompt, its options, the correct answer or answers, and an explanation. A Question belongs to exactly one Bank.
_Avoid_: Card (a Question shown in Flashcards is still a Question), item.

**Study State**: What a Learner has recorded about one Question on their own browser: whether they know it, are still learning it or haven't rated it, and whether they starred it.
_Avoid_: Progress (Progress is the tab that summarises Study State and Results), mastery.

**Deck**: A named collection of Questions from one Exam that a Learner builds for themselves. Known, Still Learning, Starred and Practice Incorrect are views over Study State, not Decks.
_Avoid_: Set, list, auto-deck.

**Quiz**: A timed or untimed practice attempt over Questions from one Exam, with answers revealed only at the end. The Learner chooses how many Questions and how long.
_Avoid_: Test, practice exam.

**Exam Simulation**: A Quiz shaped like the real Exam: its full Question count and time limit, graded pass or fail against the Exam's pass mark.
_Avoid_: Mock exam, practice test, sim (in prose).

## Usage

**Learner**: A person studying on the site, identified by the name they enter on first visit plus a random ID tied to that browser. The same person on two devices is two Learners with the same name.
_Avoid_: User, account (there is no login and no password).

**Activity**: One recorded thing a Learner did: a Visit, an Exam Opened, or a Result. Kept indefinitely and visible only to the site owner.
_Avoid_: Event, analytics, telemetry.

**Visit**: A Learner using the site on a given day. At most one per Learner per day.

**Exam Opened**: A Learner selecting an Exam to study.

**Result**: The outcome of a finished Quiz or Exam Simulation: score, pass/fail, question count, time taken.
_Avoid_: Score (a score is one part of a Result).

**Admin Page**: The owner-only page that lists Learners and their Activity. Only the site owner can sign in to it.
_Avoid_: Dashboard (the Progress tab is the Learner's own dashboard).
